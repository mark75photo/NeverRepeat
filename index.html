NeveRRepeat · Remembered Rotation
The two green R’s are the idea: RR remembers what has played so a big music library does not collapse into the same handful of songs.

Automatic Genre Runs: NeveRRepeat plays the selected Genre Run length, such as 3–6 songs in one genre, then deliberately switches to a different eligible genre for the next run. Genre and Decade filters define which songs are eligible.

20-second rule: skip before 20 seconds and the song leaves this session without being marked heard. Artist Spacing keeps the same artist apart. Genre Run plays short related runs before moving on. Discovery occasionally introduces something outside your Liked Songs. Decade + Genre narrow today’s rotation without erasing RR history. Like / Remove curates Spotify directly. Drive Mode simplifies the screen.

Progress is stored on this device. Discovery songs stay outside the main no-repeat cycle.
